# Decisions

Decisions already made, newest first, in a short ADR form. Open items are on [open questions](open-questions.md). The rules players see are on the [league pages](../league/).

## 7. Sample pairings have no rounds

- **Date:** 2026-11-26
- **Context:** With 12 players, grouping games into rounds of 3 tables forces repeat meetings: a round-2 table draws 4 players from only 3 round-1 tables. The best 3-round layout found had 3 pairs meeting in every game.
- **Decision:** Even meetings come first. The samples list games with no rounds, to be played in any order.
- **Consequence:** Scheduling is free, but nothing groups games into weeks.

## 6. Table reach at least 21

- **Date:** 2026-11-25
- **Context:** The tool used 17, the Law of Root's 2-player and "adventurous" floor. The Law recommends 21 for 4 players (5.2.1).
- **Decision:** Every table needs reach of at least 21. The tool's `MIN_REACH` is 21.
- **Consequence:** The samples still give everyone their top picks with no repeats; tables land between 21 and 28.

## 5. 8 players dropped

- **Date:** 2026-11-25
- **Context:** 8 is the most awkward count: with 3 games each, someone always gets a lopsided schedule. See [pairing approach](pairing-approach.md#player-counts).
- **Decision:** 8 players is not planned for.
- **Consequence:** Only 10 and 12 are worked through.

## 4. Rounds are a grouping, not a time slot

- **Date:** 2026-11-25
- **Context:** The one-faction and one-board limits were first read as per round.
- **Decision:** A round's games need not be played together, so those limits apply per table only.
- **Consequence:** Two games in a round can share a faction or a board.

## 3. Factions only from the player's own list

- **Date:** 2026-11-25
- **Context:** The exploratory tool allowed off-list factions as a fallback.
- **Decision:** A player only ever plays factions from their own list. This is a hard rule.
- **Consequence:** The tool does not meet the rules yet; see the [planning README](README.md).

## 2. Score is a win, 4 players a table, no Advanced Setup

- **Date:** 2026-11-25
- **Context:** Will restated the format from first principles.
- **Decision:** Only a win scores. Every table has 4 players. Factions come through the pairings from each player's list, not through Advanced Setup.
- **Consequence:** Finishing places matter only as a possible tiebreak for a final cut.

## 1. Two games at once is allowed, not optimized for

- **Date:** 2026-11-25
- **Context:** Running two tables at once had been planned around.
- **Decision:** It is allowed but is a scheduling detail, not a format rule.
- **Consequence:** Pairings ignore it.
