# Open Questions

Everything still undecided about the league, and findings that are not rules until Will confirms them. Once settled, a question moves to [decisions](decisions.md) and, if players need it, to the [league pages](../league/).

## Still open: every season

- **Seat tiebreak for factions:** what seat order should favour when two faction choices are equally good.
- **Militant and insurgent mix:** what "sensible mix" means at a table. The samples use at least one of each type, and never both Vagabond and Knaves. Each faction's type is on [factions](../league/factions.md).
- **Final table:** whether to hold one, and if so, the tiebreak order for the cut, how finalists choose seats and factions, and the map. See [final table](season-1/final-table.md).

## Still open: season 1

- **Player count:** final roster size. 12 for now, with Chuck and Corey still possible; see [roster](../season-1/roster.md).
- **Games each:** 3 or 4. There is a sample of each: [3 games](season-1/sample-pairings-3-games.md) and [4 games](season-1/sample-pairings-4-games.md).
- **Faction pool:** which factions and expansions are owned.
- **Logistics:** deck (standard or Exiles and Partisans), game time cap and the rule when it is hit, drop-out policy, prizes, dates.

## Findings to confirm

Carried over from the earlier analysis. These are findings, not rules, until Will confirms them.

- **Players × games must be a multiple of 4.** With 3 games each, that means a player count divisible by 4. 10 players works only with an even number of games each.
- **Cut ladder, if there is a final:** wins, then strength of schedule, then sum of finishing places, then a random draw. Finishing places would be recorded for tiebreaks only, never as score. Will picked this when the final was required. Check that it still stands now the final is optional.
- **Strength of schedule still earns its place.** At 12 players and 3 games it only reflects the wins of the 2 opponents a player never met, yet it settles over a third of cuts. Dropping it more than doubles the random draws; see [final table](season-1/final-table.md).
