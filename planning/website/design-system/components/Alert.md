A message with an icon and a sentence. The icon and the words say what kind it is; the tint only supports them.

- **Warning (`warn`):** a rule the person broke that they may still override.
- **Error (`bad`):** something failed or is invalid. Name what went wrong and where. Announce it with `role="alert"`.
- **Success (`ok`):** the thing is valid or done, and why.
- **Info (`info`):** a stage, a lock or a note.

Write one or two plain sentences. No apology, no vague "Something went wrong". Icons are 18px, from the Iconography table.
