A white surface with a 1px `line` border and `radius-card` corners. Cards separate objects from the `paper` ground, so use them for things people open or compare: a game, a player, a stat. Do not nest cards, and do not add a shadow (the toast is the only shadow).

- **Padding:** `.p16` for rows and game cards, `.p20` for larger blocks.
- **Stat:** `.stat` stacks a kicker, a figure (`.num`) and a one-line caption. The figure is Alegreya Sans 800 with tabular, lining figures so columns of them line up.
- **Headings** inside cards use `.disp` (IM Fell English, regular weight) with `.h2` or `.h2b` for the size.
