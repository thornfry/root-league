Inline SVG, 16 by 16 viewBox, `fill="none"`, `stroke="currentColor"`, stroke width 2, round caps and joins. They take the colour of the text beside them. Sizes: 12px in tags, 14px in buttons, 18px in alerts.

Decorative icons are `aria-hidden="true"`. An icon-only button carries an `aria-label`. Never use emoji.
The paths are in the README's Iconography table.
