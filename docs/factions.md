## Reach

From the [Law of Root, 5.2 Reach](https://www.therootdatabase.com/law/setup/en/) (page updated October 19, 2026). Reach is part of standard setup, not Advanced Setup.

- **Rule:** choose factions whose reach adds up to at least the recommended sum for the player count. Adventurous players may use any mix with 17 or more.
- **No maximum:** the Law sets no upper limit. The highest possible 4-player total is 35 (cats, rats, badgers and moles).

**5.2.1 Viable reach sums**

| Players | Reach sum |
|---:|---:|
| 2 | 17+ |
| 3 | 18+ |
| 4 | 21+ |
| 5 | 25+ |
| 6 | 28+ |
