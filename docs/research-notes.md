# Root League: Research Notes

[Document index](README.md) · [Player guide](player-guide.md)

Internal background: what other Root tournaments and leagues did, and what was learned. Findings come from web searches and page fetches in October 2026. Where a page could not be fetched, the note says so. These are precedents, not league rules; see [decisions](league-decisions.md) for what we have adopted and [variables](league-variables.md) for format analysis.

## Root tournaments

**Root Con 2023 (Portland, August 19-20).** Held at Guardian Games by Good Time Society (the Woodland War Machine podcast crew), with Leder Games and Guardian Games. Billed as the largest in-person Root tournament ever, with Cole Wehrle and Josh Yearsley attending, prizes, raffles, a live podcast recording and RPG tables. Stats from a write-up: 49 games over 5 rounds, maps pre-assigned per table, factions drafted from a pool, 14 five-player games. Faction win rates: Eyrie Dynasties 48%, Keepers in Iron 33%, Lizard Cult 31%, Woodland Alliance 26%, Underground Duchy 18%, Marquise de Cat 17%, Corvid Conspiracy 17%, Vagabond 16%, Riverfolk Company 12%, Lord of the Hundreds 10%. Seats one and four were favored, and only one fifth-seat win appeared in fourteen five-player games. Not found: how advancement worked, prizes. The BGG thread could not be fetched.

**Root Conquest (Orlando, September 5, 2026).** Single elimination, Advanced Setup (ADSET), limited faction pool of about half the lineup, drafted at each table. Three-hour rounds with 15-minute breaks, 3-4 players per table, pre-selected map, 64 spots. Stalling can bring a warning or penalty. Not a learn-to-play event. The page returned a 404 when fetched directly, so details came from search snippets.

**Montreal annual tournament.** October 2023: 43 players, 10 tables pre-designed from players' top 3 favorite factions, autumn board, standard setup and deck. Round-one losers played a custom endgame Root puzzle. May 2024: 49 players, 10 tables of 4-5, 7 tables drafted the Marauder factions, maps assigned randomly, Exiles and Partisans deck. Participants got promo cards and sheets designed by fellow players.

**Wisconsin store event (July 2025).** 16 players, 4 tables of 4, winners play a final. Random faction drawing for each game, base game only, up to about 2 hours per game, $10 entry.

**Earlier plus-one pool tournaments** (Make Craft Game statistics, nearly 60 games). The pool held one more faction than players, with the reach system choosing a satisfactory mix. Players chose maps and deck. Win rates in that data: Riverfolk 44%, Vagabond 43%, Woodland Alliance 31%, Lizard Cult and Eyrie 27%, Underground Duchy 25%, Marquise 21%, Corvid Conspiracy 7%. A faction scoring analysis tracked both draft styles and showed several factions scoring faster per round under AdSet than under the plus-one pool. Treat that as a hint that game length may differ, not a conclusion.

**Winter Tournament 2023.** A Tabletop Simulator mod with scripted setup existed. No format details found.

No documented Root league with a points table was found.

## Draft methods

**AdSet (Advanced Setup, the current official method).** From the Law of Root appendix. Choose the map, optionally the Exiles and Partisans or Squires and Disciples deck, optional bots, landmarks and hirelings. Seating and first player are random. Faction setup can use the faction setup cards from the Marauder and Homeland expansions: build a pool with one more faction card than there are players (one militant card to the center, then the remaining militant cards shuffled with the insurgent cards and one dealt per player). An insurgent card dealt last is locked until a militant faction has been taken. Vagabond cards come with a random character, Knaves with four random captains. Starting with the last player and moving counterclockwise, each player picks a card and sets up that faction immediately in flexible start positions, avoiding enemy homelands. Each player keeps three of five starting cards. Cole Wehrle describes the draft as an easy way to generate new faction configurations without the reach system, aimed at experienced players.

**Plus-one pool with reach.** Pool is one faction larger than the player count. The Law of Root asks for a minimum total reach that depends on player count (21 or more at four players per the current setup page, with 17 or more for adventurous groups) and lists each faction's reach value.

**Random assignment.** Used at the Wisconsin store event. Simple but can produce lopsided tables.

**Preference-based tables.** Used in Montreal in 2023.

## League and scoring precedents (non-Root)

- BGG Dominion League: points per game win plus bonuses for series wins, minimum games to qualify, win percentage as a tiebreaker.
- A pinball league: 8 weeks, groups of 3-4 per round, placement points, finals. A commenter showed that 5-3-2-1 scoring cannot be balanced across 3-player and 4-player groups, so group size changes expected points.
- Student Robotics league: four teams per game, descending league points, tied places split points.
- A Freeciv league discussion: per-game points favor players who play more games, so give everyone the same number of games.
