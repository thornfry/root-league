# Root League

*Eight-player format draft for player review.*

[Players](players.md) · [Standings](standings.md)

## Format

- **8 players, 4 games each.** Two tables of four play each round.
- **Rounds 1–3: qualifying.** Opponents change each round. Each win counts toward your record; other placements and VP totals do not.
- **Round 4: finals.** The top four by qualifying wins play the final; the other four play a consolation game. Strength of schedule breaks qualifying ties.
- **The final winner is league champion.** Everyone plays all four rounds.

## Setup

We propose [**Advanced Setup (AdSet)**](https://www.therootdatabase.com/law/advanced-setup/en/) for every game. You may play the same faction in multiple games.

Dates, pairings and table details will follow.
