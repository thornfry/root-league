# Sample Pairings: 4 Games

A sample layout for 12 players, 4 games each, which makes 12 games. For the 3-game version, see [sample pairings: 3 games](sample-pairings-3-games.md). For how a cut to a final table would go in either format, see [final table](final-table.md). The 12 are the 10 [confirmed players](roster.md) plus Chuck and Corey, who are still possible. It is not the real pairings. Factions come from the current [faction picks](faction-picks.md), many of which are still provisional. Players were placed into slots at random, so who gets which schedule means nothing.

There are no rounds. With 12 players, grouping the games into rounds of 3 tables forces extra repeat meetings: a round-2 table draws 4 players from only 3 round-1 tables, so two of them have met. Even meetings come first, so the games can be played in any order.

- **Meetings:** every player meets one opponent twice and the other 10 once, missing nobody. That is the best possible for 12 players and 4 games.
- **Seats:** every player sits in each seat exactly once. Seat 1 is first in turn order.
- **Maps:** each map hosts 3 games. Nobody plays a map three times. 4 players play all four maps, 6 play one map twice, and Chuck and Matt play two maps twice. Every player playing all four maps is not possible with these meetings: the fewest repeats any map plan allows is 9, and that plan puts someone on one map three times, so this plan takes 12 instead.
- **Factions:** every player plays their top 4 picks, one per game, and nobody repeats a faction. Every table has one of each faction, reach of at least 21 ([Law of Root 5.2.1](factions.md#reach)), at least one militant and one insurgent faction, and never both Vagabond and Knaves. Which of a player's top 4 lands in which game is arbitrary; the [seat tiebreak](pairing-rules.md#still-open) is still open.
