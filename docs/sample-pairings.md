# Sample Pairings

A sample layout for the 10 [confirmed players](roster.md), 4 games each, which makes 10 games. It is not the real pairings. Factions come from the current [faction picks](faction-picks.md), six of which are still provisional. Players were placed into slots at random, so who gets which schedule means nothing.

- **Seats:** every player sits in each seat exactly once. Seat 1 is first in turn order.
- **Meetings:** every player meets 3 opponents twice and the other 6 once, missing nobody. That is the best possible for 10 players and 4 games.
- **Maps:** all four are used. Winter and Autumn (the base board, several copies) host 3 games each; Lake and Mountain host 2 each. 2 players play all four maps and the other 8 play one map twice. That is the best possible for these tables, because every two games share a player.
- **Factions:** every player plays their top 4 picks, one per game, and nobody repeats a faction. Every table has one of each faction, reach of at least 21 ([Law of Root 5.2.1](factions.md#reach)), at least one militant and one insurgent faction, and never both Vagabond and Knaves. Which of a player's top 4 lands in which game is arbitrary; the [seat tiebreak](pairing-rules.md#still-open) is still open.

Rounds are a grouping, not a time slot, so the games can be played in any order. Splitting them into 5 rounds of two tables plus two byes would leave at least 5 pairs who never meet.
