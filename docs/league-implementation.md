# Root League: Implementation

[Document index](README.md) · [Player guide](player-guide.md)

Internal planning reference: how the league runs, end to end. See [variables](league-variables.md) for format options and [decisions](league-decisions.md) for confirmed rules and proposals. Items marked **TBD** are not decided yet; proposals are not confirmed rules.

## Format at a glance

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Game | Root (Leder Games), 4 players per table |
| Players in the league | 8 |
| Games per player | 4 |
| Total games | 8 (2 tables per round, 4 rounds) |
| Qualifying | Rounds 1-3, mixed pairings |
| Round 4 | Final table (top 4 by wins) and consolation table (other 4) |
| Score | A win. Nothing is awarded for other placements |
| Champion | The winner of the final table |
| Tiebreaker for the cut | Strength of schedule, then a last-resort tiebreak (TBD) |

## Rounds

### Rounds 1-3: qualifying

- Each round has two tables of four, and both run at the same time.
- Pairings should mix people as evenly as possible. Perfectly even mixing is not possible in 3 rounds (each player has 9 opponent slots but only 7 other players), so repeat opponents are expected. Strength of schedule corrects for this at the cut.
- Seat positions are balanced so that, as far as the schedule allows, nobody keeps getting the same seat. See [Root Con 2023 research](research-notes.md#root-tournaments) for the seat-balance motivation.
- The schedule is generated by script. **TBD:** generate and attach the rounds 1-3 schedule with real names.

### Round 4: final and consolation

- After round 3, rank all players by wins, then by strength of schedule.
- The top 4 play the **final table**. The other 4 play the **consolation table**.
- Both tables run at the same time. Everyone still plays their fourth game.
- The final table's winner is the league champion. The consolation winner is recognized but has no effect on the title.
- Proposed, not confirmed: final-table players choose seat and faction in standings order, so the best qualifying record gets first pick.

## Scoring and standings

- A player's record is simply their number of wins. Second place, third place and final VP are not scored, because the VP total says little about how close a game was.
- After round 3, players are ranked by wins, then **strength of schedule**. Proposed definition: total opponents' qualifying wins, counting an opponent once for each game shared. A higher total ranks higher.
- If players are still tied at the cut for the final table, use the last-resort tiebreak. **TBD:** random draw or a sudden-death game between the tied players.
- The title is decided by the final table alone, so strength of schedule is not needed after round 4.

## Table setup

These are the setup choices every table needs. Defaults come from what tournaments commonly do. **All TBD** unless noted.

- **Faction selection (proposed):** Advanced Setup (AdSet) for every game. Both tables use the same method.
- **Faction repeats (confirmed):** unrestricted. Players may play the same faction in multiple games.
- **Map:** assign in advance per table so no one has to choose. **TBD** which map(s).
- **Deck and expansions:** **TBD** (standard deck or Exiles and Partisans, which expansions are in the faction pool).
- **Dominance and coalition rules:** play the current Law of Root. A dominance win or a Vagabond coalition win counts as a win.
- **Time cap:** a cap per game is advisable. Other events use about 2 hours of expected play, and one tournament capped rounds at 3 hours. **TBD.** Also decide what happens if the cap is hit (for example, the player with the most VP wins, or the game is a draw).

## Logistics

- Two tables run at once, which means two copies of the components, or enough shared pieces to cover both. The faction pool and any drafts may need to be staggered so the same pieces are not needed at the same moment.
- With roughly 2 hours per game and two tables at a time, the 8 games take about 4 time slots, around 8 hours of table time. Rounds 1-3 are three slots, and round 4 (final and consolation together) is the fourth.
- Rounds 1-3 can be spread across sessions. Round 4 should be a single session so the final is played fresh.
