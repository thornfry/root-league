# Pick’em Rules

## Making picks

Choose a player you think will win. You can add a second pick or leave it at one. You can pick on any game you’re sitting out.

Tap a selected player again to remove that pick. If you remove your first pick, your second pick moves into first place. Picks lock when the first result is submitted or an admin settles or voids the game.

## Scoring

- A correct first pick earns **3 points**.
- A correct second pick earns **1 point**.
- If the game has two winners and you picked both, you earn **4 points**.

Disputed games score once an admin settles the result. Void games earn no points. Your leaderboard rank is based on total points, with equal scores sharing a rank.
